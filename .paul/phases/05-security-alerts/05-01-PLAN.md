<objective>
## Goal
Close the 3 open CodeQL alerts (rule `*/incomplete-url-substring-sanitization`) by validating Google Maps URLs through a parsed hostname comparison instead of `startswith`.

## Purpose
Current checks accept `https://www.google.com/maps@evil.com/...` style inputs and similar. Competitor and pharmacy URLs are sent to the scraper, so a bypass is an SSRF-adjacent risk.

## Output
- New pure-stdlib helper `backend/services/url_validation.py`
- `automation.py` and `competitor.py` validators call the helper
- `CompetitorAnalyser.tsx` uses `new URL()` hostname check
- pytest cases for the helper and both validators
</objective>
